Classic Lasagna

Description

Layers of tender pasta, slow-simmered beef ragù, creamy ricotta and bubbling mozzarella — lasagna is the ultimate comfort dish for a slow Sunday.

It takes a little patience, but every minute is rewarded with a golden, crisp-edged tray that feeds a crowd and tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 800 g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 450 g ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 400 g mozzarella, shredded
  • 60 g parmesan, grated
  • Fresh basil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and cook the noodles until al dente.
  2. Brown the beef with onion and garlic, then stir in tomatoes and paste. Simmer for 30 minutes.
  3. Mix ricotta, egg, half the parmesan and chopped basil in a bowl.
  4. Spread sauce in a baking dish, then layer noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella and sauce. Repeat three times.
  5. Top with remaining mozzarella and parmesan. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes.
  6. Uncover and bake 20 more minutes until golden. Rest 15 minutes before slicing.
