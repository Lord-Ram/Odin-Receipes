Fluffy Pancakes

Description

Tall, golden and impossibly soft, these buttermilk pancakes are the weekend breakfast everyone hopes for.

Top them with fresh berries and a generous pour of maple syrup for a stack that's as pretty as it is delicious.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Fresh berries
  • Maple syrup

Steps

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk buttermilk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla.
  3. Pour wet into dry and stir gently — a few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a buttered pan over medium heat and pour ¼ cup batter per pancake.
  5. Flip when bubbles form on top, cooking until golden on both sides.
  6. Stack high, top with berries and drizzle with maple syrup.
