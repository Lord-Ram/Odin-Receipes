Butter Chicken

Description

Butter chicken, or murgh makhani, is a beloved North Indian curry: smoky marinated chicken simmered in a velvety tomato, butter and cream sauce.

Mildly spiced and deeply aromatic, it's perfect with warm naan or fluffy basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 400 g tomato purée
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
  • Fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, half the spices and salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Sear chicken in a hot pan until charred at the edges. Set aside.
  3. Melt butter, then sauté onion until golden and add ginger-garlic paste.
  4. Add tomato purée and remaining spices; simmer 15 minutes.
  5. Blend the sauce smooth, return to the pan, and stir in cream and fenugreek.
  6. Add chicken, simmer 10 minutes, and garnish with cilantro.
